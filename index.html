Bookkeeping

Bookkeeping is a personal, self-hosted finance tool used by a single person, its author. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts or sign-ups.

With the owner's consent, it uses Google OAuth with the read-only Gmail scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly) to find e-statement emails sent by the owner's banks, download their PDF attachments, and import the transactions into a ledger that runs on the owner's own computer. It never sends, modifies, or deletes email.

繁體中文

Bookkeeping 是作者本人自用、自行架設的記帳工具，不對外提供服務，沒有使用者註冊。 經本人授權後，它以 Gmail 唯讀權限讀取本人銀行寄來的電子帳單、下載 PDF 附件，並把交易匯入本人電腦上的帳本； 不會寄信、修改或刪除任何郵件。
